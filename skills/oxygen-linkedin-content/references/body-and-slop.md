# Body and slop

Voice comes from the canonical voice page and `content-anti-patterns`. This file holds structure and gates, not a voice: never import another author's dialect, slang, number separator or signature marks.

## Find the post

A story the author has not told in public needs their yes first. A story needs a protagonist and a reversal; numbers stated as events in sequence, the last reversing the first, count as one.

## The skim test (gate)

Delete everything except line 1, the first words of each block or list head, and the last line. Read what is left.

If the skeleton still carries the argument, ship. If it reads as fragments, re-pick the structure; editing sentences will not fix it. List heads must read on their own: "1) Spec writing", not "1) The first thing".

## Shape targets

Targets, not gates: a line that breaks one and lands beats a clean one that lands nothing.

| Rule | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Ideas per line | 1. A line with two ideas gets split |
| Median line length | 40 to 50 characters |
| Longest line | never over 120 characters |
| Block | 2 lines, 3 at most once per post |
| List items | 3 to 7 |
| Devices | at most 2 (anaphora, sub-arrows, contrast pair, standalone punch, PS line) |
| Markers | one per job: `1)` steps, `->` sub-points, `-` flat lists. Never `•`, keycap or emoji bullets |

## Personal beat

Exactly one, from a public or approved source. Best: an owned mistake with the author's own bad numbers. A credibility insert ("I say this as someone who...") risks nothing and reads as AI.

## Call to action

The funnel job decides whether there is one and what it may ask for ([pillars and learning](pillars-and-learning.md)); only a real, available offer gets one. A question earns its place only when it maps to a concrete reply, not a thumbs-up.

Banned closers: `Agree?`, `Thoughts?`, `What do you think? Drop a comment`, `Tag someone who needs this.`, `Like and follow for more.`, and `Save this for later.` as the last line.

## Slop scrub (gate)

Scan before review. Any hit gets rewritten.

- Punctuation: no em dash, no curly quotes, no `…`, no Unicode fake-bold, no hashtags.
- Phrases: in today's fast-paced world, dive in, delve into, unlock the power, at its core, it's worth noting, a testament to, game changer, move the needle, here's the kicker, let that sink in, read that again, plot twist, I'm thrilled to announce, no more X, say goodbye to X, it's that simple, here's what nobody tells you, the hard truth.
- Words, replaced by the plainest one: leverage (use), utilize, empower (help), robust (strong), seamless, transformative (big), underscore (show), crucial, pivotal, holistic, streamline, elevate, harness, landscape, ecosystem, journey.
- Structure:
  - smug parallelism: a second jab that echoes the first and adds nothing;
  - a slogan that reframes the topic with rhythm instead of information, even one;
  - aphoristic closers ("Distribution is the moat."): end on practical advice, an honest precondition, or just stop;
  - the negation pivot ("It's not X. It's Y.") at most once, and only when the contrast is the story;
  - transition questions: "The result?", "The catch?", "Why does this matter?";
  - throat-clearing: Furthermore, Moreover, Additionally, Ultimately;
  - symmetric paragraphs, a recap of what the post showed, `TL;DR`, `Key takeaways`.
