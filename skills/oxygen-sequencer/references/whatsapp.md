# WhatsApp steps

A WhatsApp step (`whatsapp_message` with a `template`) sends from a connected WhatsApp account through an unofficial linked-device connection.

- Opt-in: a live start needs the Sequence's `--whatsapp-cold-initiate` permission, an explicit acceptance of ban risk for messaging people with no prior chat. Without it, `launch_readiness.blockers` names the gap. Web: Launch, Sending settings. State the risk before switching it on.
- Accounts: `oxygen whatsapp accounts --json` lists them; pass their ids to `--senders`. `whatsapp get <id>` and `whatsapp limits get <id>` show the account's warm-up ramp, daily limits and reset timezone, shared between direct sends and Sequences; `whatsapp limits set` adjusts them within safe maximums.
- Phone numbers come from the lead row (`--phone-column-key`, else `phone`, `phone_number` or `mobile`). A lead given only a phone in `--leads-file` is auto-filed into the Sequence's leads table.
- Duplicate protection defaults to **This campaign**: one recipient per number, with each step or identical message sent once even after a restart or sender change. Choose **All campaigns** in Launch → Sending settings to also block identical copy from other campaigns. CLI create/update: `--whatsapp-duplicate-scope campaign|workspace`; MCP create/update: `whatsapp_duplicate_scope`. Different follow-up copy remains eligible. An uncertain delivery stays blocked; inspect the action before any resend. Manual Messages replies and simulations are excluded.
- Warm-up: OXYGEN allows 5/day on days 0–2, 10/day on days 3–6, 15/day on days 7–13, then your configured cap (20 default, 40 maximum). This is a conservative product policy, not a guaranteed safe provider limit. New chats and Sequence outreach wait 24 hours after connecting or reconnecting; existing-thread replies and reads remain available. Read `whatsapp limits get` for `session_connected_at` and `outreach_resume_at`. Reconnection preserves the original ramp age.
- Recipient limits: a prospect confirmed to have no WhatsApp account consumes no daily campaign recipient slot when no message was sent. That pre-send skip, or a confirmed pre-send cancellation, returns its reserved first-touch slot once to the original UTC budget day. A later email or LinkedIn first contact still needs a recipient slot. The number lookup still uses read quota. An accepted or uncertain send retains capacity and blocks automatic resending. Inspect stored skips and sends with `oxygen sequences events <sequence> --kind skipped,sent --json`.
- Hours: WhatsApp accounts have no working hours of their own. The Sequence's schedule (`--schedule-*`, or `--linkedin-*` for LinkedIn and WhatsApp alone) is the only hours limit.
- Daily reset: sender quotas reset at midnight in `daily_reset_timezone`, returned by `oxygen whatsapp get <id> --json` and `oxygen whatsapp limits get <id> --json`. Campaign recipient slots reset at midnight UTC; the elapsed-day warm-up ramp is independent of both resets.
- Ceiling: a live start needs `--max-live-sends` (`SKILL.md`, Send ceilings).
- Replies: any inbound WhatsApp message stops the enrollment and, by default, adds the person to do-not-contact (`whatsapp_reply`; `SKILL.md`, Do not contact). The conversation continues in Messages (`oxygen-unibox`).

One message into a chat that already exists is a Messages action (`inbox send`, `whatsapp warm-send`); a one-to-one cold chat started by hand is `inbox start-whatsapp`.
