# A daily ranked queue of your warmest leads

For an admitted saved DEV onboarding journey, use its native read/save/advance/navigation owners before routine setup. Reuse actual paid/trial eligibility even at zero credits; exhausted funding pauses funded work without an early charge or another trial. Back and non-destructive onboarding restart preserve this play's Tables, campaigns, chats and billing. Explicit LinkedIn skip leaves connection-dependent sources unavailable; never present that as an empty successful scan. Routine preview/approval remains separate from free navigation.

## The Play
Every morning you get one short list of the people who recently showed interest in you (viewed your LinkedIn profile, followed you, reacted to your posts or visited your website), warmest first, so you reach out before they go cold. It is the right play once one of those sources is connected; until then it connects the fastest one, your LinkedIn account. Two optional habits keep it going: a capped morning briefing in the shared Workspace Oxygent chat and a five-minute weekly cleanup of duplicate contacts.

## Before You Start
- Company, offer and ICP (who you sell to) are saved: `oxygen context profile get --json` (free). Missing → tell the founder "I need one line on what you sell and to whom", then run the day-1-first-win play.
- Contact lists (the CRM) exist: `oxygen crm objects --json` (free). `objects: []` → Steps 1–2 create them; tell the founder "I'll set up your contact lists first; it's free."
- A warm source is connected: `oxygen senders list --json` (free). Connecting a LinkedIn account needs a paid plan; on Free the connect returns `upgrade_required`, so tell the founder to subscribe and link its `upgrade_url`, never a credit top-up. On a plan the account reserves 3,400 credits ($34) a month from the day it connects, and connecting needs that much in `spendable_credits`; `oxygen billing seats --json` (free) confirms how this workspace pays. Website visitors need RB2B, a separate paid tool, plus OXYGEN support to switch on the intake (no self-serve step yet; see website-visitor-outreach). None → tell the founder "Connecting your LinkedIn is the fastest way to see who is interested" and go to Step 4.
- Credits: `oxygen billing balance --json` (free; read `spendable_credits`, which comes after the six-plan price list). Reading the list is free; the only recurring costs are the LinkedIn charge and the optional routines (their exact approved caps). Short → tell the founder "The list costs nothing; the LinkedIn charge needs 3,400 credits a month and the routines can wait."

## Steps
1. Preview the contact lists (free; only when `crm objects` was empty): `oxygen crm setup --json`.
   Tell the founder: "OXYGEN keeps your contacts in lists for companies, people and deals; none exist yet."
2. Create the lists without the automatic paid lookups a plain setup would switch on (free, nothing recurring): `oxygen crm setup --live --without-enrichment --json`. Those lookups cost about 3 credits for each new person with a LinkedIn link (the US-only phone lookup stays off unless switched on, and then costs 10 only for a number it finds); the founder can switch one on later with `oxygen crm enrichment enable`.
   Tell the founder: "Your contact lists are ready, and nothing in them spends credits on its own."
3. Look for warm people (free): `oxygen signals leads-today --limit 10 --json`. People listed → Step 5. `leads: []` → `oxygen signals list --json`; no events means nothing is delivering yet → Step 4. If `senders list` already showed a LinkedIn account, skip the connect and its gate and run only the two imports.
   Tell the founder: "Nobody shows up yet because nothing is bringing them in yet; your LinkedIn is the fastest source."
> APPROVAL GATE — linkedin-connect: connecting is the founder's own LinkedIn login and starts a monthly charge of 3,400 credits until the account is disconnected (a paid seat instead only where `billing seats` does not say `seats_retired`). Say that the imports after it send nothing, cost nothing, and add each new viewer or follower to the contact lists.
4. Connect LinkedIn, then start the free imports. Send the founder to https://oxygen-agent.com/connections?filter=linkedin (or mint a 10-minute login link with `oxygen senders connect --country <their country code, e.g. US> --json`) and confirm with `oxygen senders list --json`. Then:
   ```bash
   oxygen viewers import --no-enrich --json
   oxygen followers import --json
   ```
   New profile viewers and followers become contacts and join the ranked list within hours (`oxygen viewers status --json` shows progress). LinkedIn hides private viewers. The first followers import adds every current follower, and all of them count as new for a week (they rank last). Post reactions come through the inbound-led-outbound play.
   Tell the founder: "Your first warm people should appear later today."
5. Read today's list (free): `oxygen signals leads-today --limit 10 --json` (add `--within-days 30` when it is thin). Show the top 5–10 in plain words: name, what they did (`topSignalType`), when (`lastSignalAt`) and their LinkedIn link; `oxygen crm get people <rowId> --json` adds their title. `fit` stays empty unless the person's company is in your CRM and scored (a paid enrichment that this play's lists leave off), so rank by what they did and when, and say so. Give each one an honest opening line built on a true fact, never an invented reason. Then file one note (free): `oxygen knowledge log append --event note --summary "Warm list: contacted 5 of 8, 1 reply" --json`.
   Tell the founder: "Here are today's warmest people, best first, each with a first line you can send."
6. When the founder asks for recurring work, read `oxygen onboarding activation status --json` first and reuse its Oxygent/chat/routine resource pointers. With the Oxygent handle known, `oxygen oxygents routines --slug <oxygent> --json` lists the creator's routines without reading conversation snippets. Load the exact setup guide with `oxygen skills get oxygen-onboarding --file references/daily-rhythm.md --json`. Recurring work that reports into chat uses Oxygent routines. The guided nightly-scan pilot requires an eligible dev workspace, current non-client membership, enabled pilot switches and a reviewed sourcing capability; retain the list and explain any access refusal.
   Tell the founder: "I can prepare a nightly scan and a morning briefing for your Oxygent chat; first we need the sources, timezone and caps."
> APPROVAL GATE — routine-activate: review each routine's exact native configuration and preview separately: confirmed IANA timezone, scan/briefing times, source and destination resources, positive scan lead ceiling, permitted actions, and inference/provider/child-cost caps. Suggested 02:00 and 08:30 are local times, not defaults. Valid current auto-approve authority can satisfy requested, fully configured operation approvals. Missing configuration still needs an answer. Activation schedules server work while the browser is closed until paused; new candidates remain review only and the briefing goes only to its bound Oxygent chat. Workspace chats are shared; Personal chats are creator-only.
7. Follow the setup guide through native `oxygen onboarding activation routines-preview`, which builds both drafts with exact source/configuration/resource/cap quotes. This explicit reviewed action also prepares an untouched factory draft's scoped version; preserve native review refusals for paused or customized Agents. It runs no inference and arms no schedule. Then use `oxygen onboarding activation routine-activate` for each purpose with its own revision and preview token. Reuse the activation's scan and briefing, then inspect each with `oxygen agent session get --slug <oxygent> --session <session> --json`. Pause either independently with `oxygen agent session pause --slug <oxygent> --session <session> --json`. A briefing distinguishes completed zero results from an unavailable or failed scan and links the actual source runs, charges and holds. Activation or a queued run does not prove the morning report was delivered.
   Tell the founder the exact two approved schedules, their caps and how to pause each. Confirm the persisted Oxygent-chat post when it arrives.
> APPROVAL GATE — merge-apply: a merge folds one contact into another for good (the merged one moves to row history). Show the dry-run diff, say which record you keep, and merge one confirmed pair at a time.
8. Weekly cleanup, about five minutes (free): `oxygen crm duplicates people --limit 25 --json`; open both records of a likely pair (`oxygen crm get people <row_id> --json`), preview with `oxygen crm merge people <keep_row_id> <merge_row_id> --json`, then apply with `oxygen crm merge people <keep_row_id> <merge_row_id> --live --confirm --json`. If you track deals, `oxygen crm pipeline --json` lists them by stage but has no last-activity date yet, so check a suspect deal with `oxygen crm activity timeline deals <row_id> --limit 1 --json`. Finish with `oxygen runs list --limit 20 --json` for anything that failed, and file a dated note.
   Tell the founder: "Your contact list is clean this week: N duplicates merged and nothing failed."

## If It Goes Wrong
- `crm_object_not_found` (`object: people`) from `signals list` or `signals leads-today` → the contact lists do not exist yet: Steps 1–2.
- `signals registry` calls every type `live` → that is what OXYGEN can capture, not what you connected; check `oxygen senders list --json`.
- `leads: []` after connecting → the imports are still dripping in: `oxygen viewers status --json` (`waiting` is LinkedIn's daily read limit, not a fault; `stalled` comes with a `health_note` naming the repair).
- `upgrade_required`, `sending_seat_required` or `insufficient_credits` while connecting LinkedIn → the plan, a seat or credits are missing; the error names the price; buy nothing without the founder's yes.
- Routine access or sourcing unavailable → retain the reviewed list and setup draft; show the native refusal and missing admission or source.
- Routine preview stale → re-read the configuration, preview again and review changed resources, timezone, actions or caps.
- Scan missing, failed, partial or still running → the briefing says that explicitly; offer a separately previewed bounded Run now when supported.
- `crm_merge_preview_stale` → a record changed after the preview; run the dry-run merge again and confirm right away.
